THE NATIONAL FREE FIRE AUTHORITY

ELA ESPORTS is India's premier Free Fire esports organization, serving as the National Free Fire Authority. We operate a structured, transparent, and community-backed competitive network for professional athletes across the nation.

Backed by the FFM Community, we provide official tournament management, verified match officials, and a comprehensive player verification system that ensures fair play and competitive integrity.

OUR MISSION

To establish India as a global leader in Free Fire esports by creating a professional, transparent, and inclusive competitive environment for all players.

OUR VISION

To build the most trusted and respected esports organization in India, fostering growth, innovation, and excellence in competitive gaming.
